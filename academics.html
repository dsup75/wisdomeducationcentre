Comprehensive Curriculum

Academics & Skill Programs

Nurturing young minds through structured ICSE foundational schooling at Naubasta and unlocking career readiness through IT vocational training at Khandepur.

Naubasta / Saraswati Nagar Branch

Class 1 to 8 ICSE Schooling

English-medium curriculum focused on holistic development, critical reasoning, and moral values.

Primary Wing

Classes 1 – 3

Foundational & Activity-Based Learning

  • English: Phonics, reading comprehension, handwriting & basic vocabulary.
  • Mathematics: Mental arithmetic, shapes, patterns, and number skills.
  • Environmental Studies: Nature observation, family, and health habits.
  • Hindi: Varnamala, matras, rhymes, and spoken conversation.
  • Creative Expression: Art, drawing, music, and physical education.
Middle Prep

Classes 4 – 5

Conceptual Depth & Analytical Inquiry

  • English: Grammar rules, paragraph writing, and guided speech.
  • Mathematics: Fractions, decimals, geometry, and real-life problem solving.
  • General Science: Living organisms, human body systems, and simple experiments.
  • Social Studies: Maps, Indian culture, continents, and historical stories.
  • Computer Studies: Introduction to computer hardware, MS Paint & Word basics.
Junior High School

Classes 6 – 8

ICSE Curriculum & High School Readiness

  • ICSE Sciences: Split subjects: Physics, Chemistry & Biology with lab demonstrations.
  • Advanced Mathematics: Algebra, Commercial Math, Geometry & Mensuration.
  • Languages: English Literature & Language, Hindi, and introductory Sanskrit.
  • History & Civics / Geography: World climates, topography, and constitution basics.
  • Computer Science: Basic programming concepts, computational logic, and internet tools.
