About Wisdom Education Centre

Fostering academic excellence and technical skills across two branches in Kanpur.

Our Educational Vision

Wisdom Education Centre was established to offer children in Kanpur a balanced blend of rigorous ICSE-oriented curriculum and practical value-based education. At our Hamirpur Road campus in Saraswati Nagar, Naubasta, we prepare children from Class 1 to Class 8 to be inquisitive thinkers and disciplined citizens.

Our Khandepur Chauraha skill facility bridges the technical divide, training students in modern computers, management foundations, and vocational skills.

Mission & Values

To provide affordable, top-quality English-medium education, respect for cultural festivals, and industry-oriented vocational skills.
